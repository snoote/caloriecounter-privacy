Common questions

  • How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device? Open Settings in the app and tap "You have Pro" (or "Get Pro" → Restore Purchases) while signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.
  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription? In the app: Settings → tap your Pro status → manage subscription. You can also do this directly in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Where is my data stored? See our Privacy Policy.
