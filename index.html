Privacy Policy — CalorieCounter

Last updated: 2 September 2026

CalorieCounter (the “app”) is an iOS calorie tracker. This policy explains what data the app processes and how.

Data controller

The app’s developer (“we”). Privacy contact: edgarakinis@gmail.com.

What data is processed

Everything you enter — food entries, daily goal, resting energy, body metrics (sex, weight, height, age), active calories — is stored only on your device (in the standard iOS preferences store). We do not receive this data, do not store it on our servers, and do not share it with third parties.

Apple Health

If you enable “From Apple Health” in Settings, the app — with your permission — reads the “Active Energy” metric from Apple Health to auto-fill your daily active calories. This data:

  • is used only to compute your calorie deficit/surplus inside the app;
  • never leaves your device, is not sent to us or any third party;
  • is not used for advertising and is not sold;
  • stops being requested as soon as you turn Apple Health mode off.

The app does not write any data to Apple Health.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan product barcodes. Camera images are not saved or transmitted anywhere.

Open Food Facts (third party)

When you search for a product by name or barcode, the app sends the entered text or barcode to the open Open Food Facts database (world.openfoodfacts.org) and receives the product’s nutrition data. The request contains no name, device identifier, or health data. Open Food Facts privacy policy: world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

What the app does NOT do

  • No analytics or tracking.
  • No advertising.
  • No accounts, no contact-data collection.

Storage and deletion

Data lives on the device while the app is installed. Deleting the app deletes all data. No deletion request is needed since we hold none of your data.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing to fulfil on our side regarding access, rectification, or erasure. For any questions: edgarakinis@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If the policy changes, we will update the date at the top and the text on this page.
